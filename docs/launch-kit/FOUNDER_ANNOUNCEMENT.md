# Founder launch announcement

> DRAFT - NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION
> Owner: Cameron Ashley | Intended surface: founder profile

Alberta clinic billing teams make consequential claim decisions under time
pressure. The hard part is not simply finding another rule; it is bringing the
right context into a review while keeping an accountable person in control.

That is the workflow I am building Zorva around: human-reviewed, pre-submit
AHCIP claim review for Alberta clinics. A billing team can examine the available
context and supporting references before deciding what, if anything, should be
submitted. Zorva does not submit the claim or replace billing judgment.
[ZC-001, ZC-002, ZC-003]

I am preparing a small, controlled pilot and speaking with Alberta practice
managers and billing leads about where review work is difficult today. The
offer, terms, and production-data prerequisites remain under review; this is a
request for a conversation rather than open self-serve access. [ZC-004]

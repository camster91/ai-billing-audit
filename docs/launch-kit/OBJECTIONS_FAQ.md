# Short objection and FAQ sheet

> DRAFT - NOT APPROVED FOR EXTERNAL USE
> Owner: Cameron Ashley | Privacy, offer, and claim review required

## Does Zorva submit claims for us?

No. The intended workflow is pre-submit review. The clinic's authorized billing
team keeps the final decision about what, if anything, is submitted. [ZC-002]

## Is it replacing our biller?

No. It is designed to place review context and supporting references beside the
encounter so a biller can assess the next action. It does not replace billing or
clinical judgment. [ZC-003]

## What results can you guarantee?

None. There is no approved savings, recovery, accuracy, denial-reduction, or
return-on-investment promise. A controlled pilot would need approved success and
stop criteria before it begins.

## What does the pilot cost and how long is it?

Those terms are not approved. Do not quote a price, duration, volume, discount,
refund, or service level until issue #76 contains the owner's decision.

## Is Zorva compliant or certified?

This draft makes no certification or compliance claim. A clinic's privacy and
security reviewers must examine the approved evidence and contractual terms
before clinic data is introduced. Do not substitute a product demo for that
review.

## Where is data stored?

No location claim is approved for this kit. Answer only from current,
contract-backed deployment evidence after the privacy owner approves the exact
wording.

## What information do you need for a first conversation?

Non-clinical workflow context: who owns review, where context is assembled, what
creates rework, which trust reviewers are involved, and whether a controlled
evaluation could meet the qualification boundary. Do not email patient or claim
information. [ZC-005]

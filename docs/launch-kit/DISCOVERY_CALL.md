# Discovery-call agenda and qualification questions

> DRAFT - INTERNAL PREPARATION ONLY
> Owner: Cameron Ashley | Do not record PHI in Zorva HQ

## Purpose

Determine whether there is a real, bounded pre-submit review problem, whether
Zorva's human-reviewed Alberta AHCIP direction is relevant, and whether both
sides can meet the privacy and operating prerequisites for a controlled pilot.
[ZC-001, ZC-005]

## 30-minute agenda

1. Context and permission to take non-clinical notes - 2 minutes
2. Current workflow and ownership - 10 minutes
3. Review friction and desired evidence - 8 minutes
4. Fit, boundaries, privacy, and onboarding - 7 minutes
5. Confirm next action or close as not now - 3 minutes

## Questions

### Current workflow

- Walk me through what happens between a completed encounter and claim
  submission.
- Which person or role makes the final billing decision?
- What triggers a second review today?
- Where does the reviewer look for supporting context?
- Which handoffs create delay or repeated work?

### Problem evidence

- How often does the team revisit a claim before submission? If unknown, say
  unknown rather than estimate.
- What makes a review item trustworthy enough to act on?
- What would make a review suggestion unusable or unsafe?
- How would the team recognize that a controlled evaluation helped its workflow
  without relying on a savings guarantee?

### Fit and boundary

- Is the clinic in Alberta and billing AHCIP through a primary-care workflow?
- Is there a named person who would own review and retain the final decision?
  [ZC-002, ZC-005]
- Is the clinic seeking advice before submission, rather than autonomous claim
  submission?
- Can the clinic complete its privacy, security, and onboarding review before
  any clinic data is introduced?
- Which decision-makers and trust reviewers must participate?

### Next action

- If there is fit: name one owner, dated next action, required evidence, and
  unresolved approval. Do not promise terms that issue #76 has not approved.
- If there is no fit: record a controlled reason such as unsupported market,
  autonomous submission required, no workflow owner, privacy prerequisites not
  possible, or timing.

## Never collect in the commercial record

Patient names or identifiers, clinical notes, diagnoses, claim narratives,
screenshots containing PHI, credentials, or production access details. Stop and
route through the approved privacy process if any appear.

## A practical review step before an Alberta claim is submitted

Zorva is designed for Alberta primary-care billing teams that want a more
consistent way to examine potential AHCIP claim problems before submission.
It supports a human-reviewed workflow: the clinic's billing team keeps the
final decision about what, if anything, is submitted. [ZC-001, ZC-002]

### The workflow

1. **Bring an encounter into review.** Start with the encounter narrative and
   billed services the team wants to assess.
2. **Review the available context.** Place relevant billing context and
   supporting references beside the encounter so the biller can assess the
   next action. [ZC-003]
3. **Make the decision as a team.** Accept, correct, dismiss, or investigate;
   Zorva does not replace billing judgment or submit the claim. [ZC-002]

### Who the controlled pilot is intended to explore fit with

- Alberta primary-care teams using AHCIP
- a named billing-workflow owner
- a human reviewer who remains responsible for submission decisions
- willingness to complete privacy and onboarding review before clinic data is
  introduced [ZC-005]

The pilot's duration, volume, pricing, success criteria, privacy terms, and
support commitments are not yet approved and are deliberately omitted.

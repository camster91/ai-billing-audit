## The situation

Alberta clinic billing teams make detailed AHCIP claim decisions under time
pressure. The information needed for a deliberate pre-submit review can be
spread across the encounter narrative, billed services, current guidance, and
the team's own operating knowledge. Zorva begins with that workflow problem. It
does not assume a clinic has a proven revenue loss, denial problem, or automation
need before discovery establishes one.

## The role Zorva is designed to play

Zorva is an Alberta-first, human-reviewed pre-submit claim-review workflow. It
is designed to place available billing context and supporting references beside
an encounter so an authorized biller can assess the next action. The clinic
keeps the final decision about what, if anything, is submitted.

## Mission

Help Alberta billing teams make better-supported pre-submit decisions while
keeping accountable people in control.

## Direction

Earn the right to become a trusted review layer for medical billing by proving
one governed Alberta workflow first. Expansion to other provinces, payers,
automation levels, or broader intelligence claims requires representative
product evidence, customer evidence, privacy review, and an explicit strategy
decision.

## What the narrative does not claim

This narrative is not evidence that Zorva finds every issue, improves revenue,
reduces denials, learns from customer data, meets a certification or regulatory
standard, operates in a particular data region, or is ready for self-serve
production use. Metrics, customer outcomes, privacy/security representations,
pricing, and service commitments require their own approved claim records.

## Short forms

- **Category:** human-reviewed pre-submit AHCIP claim review.
- **Positioning line:** Review Alberta claims with your billing team in control.
- **Plain-language promise:** Bring the available context into review and keep
  the final submission decision with the clinic.
- **Primary CTA:** Start a conversation.

These lines may be used only on surfaces allowed by the current public-claim and
content-approval process. They do not authorize publication or outreach.

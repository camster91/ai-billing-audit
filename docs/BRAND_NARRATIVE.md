# Zorva — brand narrative

## Origin

Medical billing errors cost the North American system tens of billions of dollars a year, and the cost falls on the people least able to fix it. Doctors went to medical school to care for patients, not to memorise which modifier unlocks which procedure code. Billers went to work to chase denials, not to scan a 600-line clinical note for the under-coded E/M visit hidden in the third paragraph. The expertise to catch both kinds of mistake — the wrong code and the missing one — exists nowhere in the workflow today, so the money leaks out the bottom of the practice while everyone is busy doing their actual job.

## Mission

Zorva catches what is wrong and finds what is missing. It reads every clinical note the way a careful senior biller would, flags the claim errors that lead to denials, and surfaces the under-coded services and unbilled procedures that lead to lost revenue. The biller stays in the loop, makes the final call, and gets the credit for the catch.

## Vision

A self-improving billing intelligence layer sitting between every practice and every payer — one that learns from every accept, every dismissal, and every correction, and gets measurably better at catching the next dollar for every clinic it touches.

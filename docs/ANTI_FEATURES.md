# What Zorva doesn't do (the anti-feature list)

> Sales-defense one-pager. Source of truth for "we don't do this" — when a
> prospect asks for a feature that's outside our scope, point them here.
> Marketing copy in `apps/portal/src/app/what-zorva-finds/` and the pricing
> page link to this doc as the canonical answer.

**TL;DR:** Zorva is a pre-submit **claims audit**, not a billing system, not a
clearinghouse, and not an EHR. We tell you which claims are risky; we never
submit claims, store clinical records long-term, or replace your billing
team's judgment.

## 1. We don't submit claims

Zorva audits claims **before** they go to the payer. The biller (or your
billing software) is still the one that submits. We don't connect to the
payer's submission API, we don't generate an 837P file, and we don't
negotiate with the payer on your behalf.

**Why:** Connecting to a payer submission API pulls us into a regulated
clearinghouse role (often requiring a separate certification or contract).
Our entire value is in the **review step**, not the transmission step.

**If a prospect wants this:** refer them to their existing billing
software (e.g. Telus PS Suite, Oscar, AdvancedMD) or to a clearinghouse
specialist. We integrate with their output via CSV / 837P import.

## 2. We don't store clinical notes long-term

Zorva ingests the clinical note and claim data **only for the duration of
the audit** plus a configurable retention window (default 30 days, max
90 days unless the pilot agreement says otherwise). After the retention
window, the note is deleted from our primary store and the encrypted
backup is rotated out within 7 days.

**Why:** Clinical notes are the most sensitive class of PHI under HIPAA,
PHIPA, and HIA. The smaller the retention window, the smaller the blast
radius of any breach.

**If a prospect wants this:** see `docs/DATA_AGREEMENT_TEMPLATE.md` for
the configurable retention window and `docs/BAA_TEMPLATE_HIPAA.md` (or
the PHIPA / HIA equivalents) for the legal commitment.

## 3. We don't replace your biller's judgment

Every Zorva finding shows up as a **flag on the dashboard**. The biller
sees the flag, sees the rule citation, and decides to **accept, edit, or
dismiss**. Zorva never silently edits a claim. We never auto-submit a
changed claim. The biller is always the human in the loop.

**Why:** The legal liability for the submitted claim always rests with
the biller / clinic, not with the audit vendor. Silent auto-correction
would shift that liability onto us in a way we can't insure against.

**If a prospect wants this:** direct them to a RCM (revenue-cycle
management) service for fully-automated submission, not to a pre-submit
audit.

## 4. We don't train our model on your data

Zorva's auditor model is trained on a **synthetic corpus** generated by
`src/ai_billing_audit/synth_agent.py` (see `docs/CODE_REVIEW_synth.md`).
Your real claims and findings **never** enter the training set. We don't
do "active learning" on customer data. We don't co-train across tenants.

**Why:** Cross-tenant training is both a privacy red line (PHI leaks
across tenants in a way that's hard to detect) and a competitive
liability (a clinic would rightfully object to their claim mix being used
to train a model sold to their competitor).

**If a prospect asks "but how does the model improve?":** The model
improves by (a) adding more synthetic data covering rule families we
haven't seen enough of, (b) calibrating the per-rule recall (see
`docs/RECALL_DIAGNOSIS.md`), and (c) iterating prompt variants against
the held-out test set (see `docs/ITERATION_GUIDE.md`).

## 5. We don't do clinical decision support

Zorva audits **coding and billing compliance**, not medical necessity or
clinical correctness. We don't tell the doctor whether the procedure was
the right thing to do medically. We tell the biller whether the claim as
coded is consistent with the documentation and the rules.

**Why:** Clinical decision support is a separate FDA-regulated (in the
US) or Health Canada–regulated (in Canada) category of software with its
own certification, audit trail, and liability regime.

**If a prospect wants this:** refer them to a CDS vendor (e.g. Wolters
Kluwer, EBSCO, or their EHR vendor's CDS module).

## 6. We don't do patient scheduling, intake, or messaging

Zorva doesn't talk to patients. We don't send appointment reminders, we
don't collect intake forms, and we don't have a patient portal.

**Why:** Patient-facing features are a separate product category with a
different security and privacy profile, and a different buyer (the
clinic manager vs. the billing lead).

## 7. We don't promise a specific revenue lift

We can show our historical precision and recall on the held-out test
set (see `docs/ACCEPTANCE_RUN.md`), and we can show the per-clinic
finding volume during the pilot. We **don't** promise that adopting
Zorva will increase revenue by X% — every clinic's claim mix is
different, and the relationship between caught findings and billed
revenue depends on the clinic's billing team's follow-through.

**Why:** Promising specific revenue lift invites bad-faith selling and
disappointed customers. The honest claim is "we find more findings than
your current process; whether those findings turn into billed revenue
depends on your team's review and follow-up."

## 8. We don't do denial management

Zorva runs **before** the claim is submitted. We don't have visibility
into a denial after the fact, and we don't help with appeals.

**Why:** Denial management is a different product (often called "denial
tracking" or "denial analytics"), usually sold to the same buyer but
sitting downstream of submission.

**If a prospect asks for this:** see the appeal-letter generator at
`src/ai_billing_audit/appeal_letter.py` for a starting point; we may add
a denial-tracking feature later, but it's not in scope for the pilot.

## 9. We don't integrate directly with every EHR

For the pilot, Zorva accepts 837P files, CSV uploads, and FHIR `Claim`
resources. We do not (yet) have direct, certified integrations with
every EHR. The most-common integration requests are tracked under
kanban `pilot-ready` task C7 (AdvancedMD); see
`docs/EHR_INTEGRATION_ROADMAP.md` for the current list.

**Why:** Each certified EHR integration is a 2-4 week engineering
project with a separate vendor contract. We prioritize by pilot
demand.

## 10. We don't sell your data

We don't sell, share, or resell any pilot data, audit findings, or
clinic metadata. The only data we keep is what we need to run the
service for the duration of the pilot, and (with explicit written
consent) aggregated, fully-de-identified benchmarks (e.g. "across 10
Ontario primary-care pilots, modifier-25 was the most-frequently-flagged
rule family in Q3 2026"). No re-identifiable data ever leaves the
tenant boundary.

---

## How to use this list

When a prospect asks "do you do X?" and X is on this list, link them
here and offer the alternative vendor in the "If a prospect wants this"
section. The goal is to be honest about scope, not to stretch the
pitch to fit.

If a prospect asks for something that's **not** on this list, that's a
real product feedback signal — log it on the kanban
`product-feedback` board with the prospect's name (with consent) so it
gets triaged against the roadmap.

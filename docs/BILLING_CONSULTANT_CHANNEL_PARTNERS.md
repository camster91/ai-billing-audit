## TL;DR

- **Target profile:** Independent billing consultants and small billing
  firms (1–10 people) who already serve 5+ primary-care or specialty
  clinics in Ontario or Alberta.
- **Why this channel:** A billing consultant already has the trust of
  the clinics they'd refer to us. A warm intro from a consultant
  closes 3-5x faster than cold outreach to the clinic directly.
- **Top three targets (Ontario + Alberta combined):**
  1. **Ontario Medical Billing Services** (Toronto) — long-standing
     OHIP billing consultancy, serves ~40 clinics.
  2. **K&M Medical Billing** (Calgary) — Alberta AHCIP specialist, ~25
     clinics.
  3. **Precision Medical Billing** (Toronto) — small OHIP consultancy,
     ~15 clinics, modern digital stack.

## 1. Target profile

A billing consultant is worth our time if they meet **all three** of:

1. **5+ active clinic clients** in our target specialties (primary
   care, cardiology, dermatology, internal med, OB/GYN).
2. **Handle OHIP or AHCIP billing** (not WCB-only, not US RCM).
3. **Independent** (not an in-house biller at a clinic; the channel
   partner is the one who decides which tools to recommend).

Nice-to-haves:

- A newsletter, blog, or LinkedIn presence we can co-market with.
- Existing relationships with one of our priority clinics.
- Familiarity with our competitors (so they can position us
  honestly against them).

## 3. Channel-partner economics

Two structures we'll consider; final choice is Cam's call after the
first 2-3 intros:

### 3.1 Referral fee (one-time, per closed pilot)

- **Trigger:** a referred clinic signs a paid engagement (any tier).
- **Amount:** 10% of the first 3 months' revenue. So:
  - Starter ($499/mo) → ~$150 paid to the referrer.
  - Growth ($1,499/mo) → ~$450 paid.
  - Scale ($2,999/mo) → ~$900 paid.
- **Cap:** no cap, but referral fees sunset at month 3 of the
  engagement (the consultant is paid for the introduction, not for
  ongoing retention).
- **Contract:** simple one-page referral agreement, not a full
  channel-partner contract.

### 3.2 Channel-partner agreement (recurring, longer-term)

- **Trigger:** the consultant signs 3+ clinics in 12 months.
- **Amount:** 15% recurring on all referred clinics' MRR, for the
  life of the engagement.
- **Co-marketing:** quarterly case-study write-ups (with the
  referred clinics' consent) and a Zorva logo on the consultant's
  partner page.
- **Contract:** proper channel-partner agreement with mutual
  indemnity, marketing commitments, and termination clauses.

For the first 2-3 intros we do the simpler referral fee (3.1). Once
the relationship proves out, we move the consultant to 3.2.

## 4. Outreach sequencing

For each priority consultant:

1. **Day 0** — Internal check: confirm no prior touchpoint in CRM.
2. **Day 1** — Send a tailored cold email (template at
   `templates/email/consultant_outreach_v1.txt`). Lead with the
   "you already have the trust of the clinics we'd be talking to"
   angle; that's what makes consultants listen.
3. **Day 4** — If no reply, brief follow-up.
4. **Day 7** — If a reply, book a 20-minute call (slightly longer
   than the clinic onboarding call because we need to walk through
   the referral economics).

Target: 3 consultants in this list, ~2 replies expected, ~1 active
referral relationship within 60 days.

## 5. What NOT to do

- **Do not lead with pricing or commission rates.** Lead with the
  value (they get a tool their clinics actually want) and bring up
  the economics on the call.
- **Do not promise exclusivity.** We don't give any consultant
  exclusive rights in a region — it creates bad dynamics with the
  clinics and bad dynamics with the consultants.
- **Do not skip the referral agreement.** Even a one-time referral
  fee should be on paper; a verbal "send them my way" is not
  enough if a billing dispute arises later.

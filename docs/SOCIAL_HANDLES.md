# Zorva Social Media Handle Reservation List

**Owner:** Cam (founder) — accounts created on a personal email at
registration, then ownership transferred to a shared `socials@zorva.ca`
inbox once verified.

**Status legend:**
- `RESERVED` — account created, profile picture + bio placeholder
  installed, link back to `https://zorva.ca` set. Awaiting first
  scheduled post.
- `CLAIMED` — handle held but profile is empty / unconfigured. Needs
  the `RESERVED` upgrade before going live.
- `PENDING` — application submitted, awaiting platform review (some
  brand-name handles go through manual approval).
- `SQUATTER` — third party holds the handle. Tracked for potential
  acquisition or UDRP discussion.

**Brand names to register (in order of priority):**

The brand "Zorva" is short, distinctive, and on its own works on every
platform. The longer variants below (`zorvahealth`, `zorvaai`) are
defensive — they pre-empt a future comment like "is this the same
company as @zorvahealth on Instagram?" from a confused prospect, and
they cost almost nothing to reserve.

1. `@zorva` — primary. This is the handle every external post and bio
   link should reference.
2. `@zorvahealth` — defensive, vertical-specific. Use if we ever spin
   out a consumer-facing surface or a separate product line for
   non-Alberta regions where "Zorva Health" makes more sense than the
   bare brand.
3. `@zorvaai` — defensive, technical-specific. Useful for the
   developer-relations side (API changelog posts, OSS releases) where
   the audience is more technical and "AI" in the handle is a feature
   not a bug.

We do NOT register `@zorvaofficial` or `@zorvareal` — those are
inauthentic signals and Twitter/X and Instagram have started surfacing
"official" labels natively for verified accounts.

## Account ownership + 2FA

- **Email:** all accounts registered to `socials@zorva.ca` (Google
  Workspace, 2FA required, recovery codes stored in 1Password under
  "Zorva Socials").
- **Passwords:** unique 20+ char generated by 1Password. No reuse.
  Rotated on a 90-day calendar; the first rotation is logged in
  `docs/SOCIAL_HANDLES.md` under "Rotation log" below.
- **2FA:** TOTP preferred (Authy or Google Authenticator backup).
  SMS is the fallback only if TOTP isn't supported (TikTok in some
  regions only offers SMS).
- **Recovery codes:** printed and stored in a sealed envelope in the
  fireproof safe at the founder's office. Envelope is labeled
  "ZORVA SOCIALS RECOVERY — DO NOT OPEN UNLESS LOCKED OUT".

## Trademark + brand-name protection

The Zorva mark is not yet registered with CIPO. Filing a Canadian
trademark application is on the Q1 2027 roadmap. Until then, the
handle reservations above are the cheapest defensive layer we have
against a competitor squatting the name on a platform we want to
join later.

If a `SQUATTER` handle becomes strategically important (i.e. we
decide to launch on TikTok and `@zorvaai` is a meaningful lost
opportunity), file a UDRP / platform-specific trademark complaint
ONLY after the CIPO registration clears. Platforms generally side
with trademark holders in those disputes, and they are cheap
($1,500-$3,000 through a trademark lawyer) once the mark is
registered.

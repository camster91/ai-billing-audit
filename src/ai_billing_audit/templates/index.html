How we calculated this

Revenue confirmed clean is the number of claims the auditor marked clean ({{ metrics.this_month_clean }}) multiplied by an industry-average claim value of ${{ "{:,.0f}".format(metrics.avg_claim_value_usd) }}. Production deployments will replace this with the clinic's actual average per claim, computed from their own billing data.

Estimated denials avoided compares your clean rate to the industry baseline of 92.5% (a 7.5% denial rate is the CMS-reported average for commercial payers). The difference is the share of claims your team is catching that would have been denied without the auditor, multiplied by the average claim value. {% if metrics.this_month_clean_rate is not none %} Math: ({{ (metrics.this_month_clean_rate * 100) | round(1) }}% − 92.5%) × {{ metrics.this_month_count }} × ${{ "{:,.0f}".format(metrics.avg_claim_value_usd) }} = ${{ "{:,.0f}".format(metrics.this_month_avoided_denials_dollar) }}. {% endif %}

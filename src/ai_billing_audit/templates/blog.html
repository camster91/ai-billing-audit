Why we built Zorva (and not a billing service)

Most AHCIP billing software falls into one of two categories: a managed submission service (Dr. Bill, Petal), or an EMR billing module (TELUS PS Suite, OSCAR Pro, Accuro). Neither of them reads the clinical note against the AHCIP fee schedule. We built one that does.

Why we charge flat-fee, not per-claim

Per-claim pricing punishes the clinics that need the most help. The clinics with the highest denial rates pay the most for a tool that should be making their lives easier. We picked flat-fee because the alternative rewards us for not running the auditor on the trickiest claims.

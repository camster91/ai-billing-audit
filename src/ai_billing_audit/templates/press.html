Long (1 paragraph)

Zorva is a pre-submit AHCIP audit tool for Alberta clinics. Every claim goes through the auditor before it leaves the biller's desk, catching the missed codes, underbilled modifiers, and shadow-billed services that quietly drain monthly revenue. The auditor reads the clinical note, the billed claim, and the applicable AHCIP rules and emits a structured finding list — the biller accepts the real ones and dismisses the false positives. Zorva is built and operated by Ashbi Design, a Toronto studio. The product launched in 2026 and is currently in pilot with two Alberta clinics.

The story

Zorva started as a small internal tool inside Ashbi Design — we noticed that the same 5-10 AHCIP billing patterns were causing the same 5-10 denials, on every engagement we did with Alberta clinics. We tried every off-the-shelf billing tool on the market. None of them read the clinical note against the AHCIP fee schedule. None of them caught the modifier-25 problem. None of them surfaced the CMGP underpayment.

So we built one. v12 of the Zorva prompt ships with 18 AHCIP rules — every one of them something an Alberta biller needs to know about every claim. The auditor runs at F1=0.690 on the cleaned AHCIP val set. We're working on the next iteration.

We are not trying to be the largest billing tool. We are trying to be the one Alberta billers keep open in a tab all day.

Zorva v12 — the auditor

The auditor is a large language model, prompted to read the clinical note + the billed claim + the applicable AHCIP rules and emit a structured finding list. The model is brought in by the customer — Zorva ships the prompt, the rules, the audit trail, and the biller's review UI. Your data does not train anyone else's model.

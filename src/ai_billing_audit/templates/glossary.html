Glossary

The acronyms and terms that show up on the security page, the privacy officer's brief, and the biller's day-to-day work. For the new hire who's never seen a SOMB.

How Zorva works →
AHCIP — Alberta Health Care Insurance Plan

The publicly-funded health insurance plan for Alberta residents, administered by Alberta Health. Physicians submit claims to AHCIP for the insured services they provide. Zorva is built around the AHCIP fee schedule (SOMB).

AWV — Annual Wellness Visit

A preventive-medicine service that some Alberta physicians bill as part of a comprehensive visit. AHCIP covers AWV under specific billing rules; Zorva flags when a visit looks like an AWV but isn't billed as one (and the reverse).

CMGP — Comprehensive Care Management / General Practice

A modifier that a GP appends to a same-day visit when they perform a procedure. Without CMGP, the procedure line pays at 100% but the visit line is reduced to 50% under AHCIP GR 4.4. Zorva flags when CMGP is missing on a same-day GP + procedure.

CPT — Current Procedural Terminology

The American Medical Association's billing code taxonomy. Zorva accepts CPT codes in the 837P payload but the v12 ruleset is calibrated against SOMB procedure codes, not CPT. US pilots use CPT.

EM / E/M — Evaluation and Management

A family of billing codes for office visits. The "level" (1-5) reflects complexity — history, exam, medical decision-making. Zorva flags under-billed E/M levels when the clinical note supports a higher level.

FHIR — Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources

A modern healthcare data interchange standard. The 837P is the legacy format; FHIR is the new one. Zorva accepts 837P today; FHIR R4 is on the 2026-Q4 roadmap.

GR — General Rule (AHCIP GR)

The numbered rules in the AHCIP Schedule of Medical Benefits. GR 1.4 is the modifier-25 unlock rule; GR 4.4 is the CMGP / same-day-procedure rule. Zorva cites the GR number for every finding.

HIA — Alberta Health Information Act

Alberta's health-privacy law. Governs how custodians (physicians, hospitals, PCNs) handle health information and who they can share it with. Zorva signs an Information Manager Agreement (IMA) with every Alberta clinic before any data is accepted.

HIPAA — Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act

The US federal health-privacy law. Zorva is Alberta- first; HIPAA BAA templates are on request for US pilots. Not pre-signed for jurisdictions where we don't currently have a customer.

H-Link — Alberta Health's electronic claims system

The submitter-portal that clinics use to upload 837P claims to AHCIP. Zorva is pre-submit — we audit before the biller uploads to H-Link. We don't integrate with H-Link directly; the biller does.

HMV — Health Maintenance Visit

A specific preventive visit type in AHCIP, distinct from a regular office visit. Zorva flags when a visit looks like a candidate HMV but isn't billed as one.

IMA — Information Manager Agreement

The HIA-required contract between a custodian (a clinic) and an information manager (Zorva). Sets out what Zorva can and can't do with the data. Zorva's IMA template is in lawyer review (target sign-off 2026-Q3).

LMM — Large Language Model

The AI class of model that Zorva uses for the audit. Brought in by the customer — Zorva ships the prompt, the rules, the audit trail. Your data does not train anyone else's model.

MUE — Medically Unlikely Edit

A payer rule that caps the number of units of a procedure that can be billed in a day. Zorva flags when a claim's units exceed the MUE limit.

NCCI — National Correct Coding Initiative

A US-based code-pair edit system. Less directly relevant to AHCIP (which has its own equivalent in the SOMB) but Zorva's NCCI coverage is wired in for US pilots.

PCN — Primary Care Network

Alberta-specific organizational structure that coordinates billing, panel management, and after-hours coverage across member clinics. ~42 PCNs province- wide. The PCN central office is often the right first call for Zorva outreach.

PHIPA — Personal Health Information Protection Act (Ontario)

Ontario's health-privacy law. Distinct from HIA in that PHIPA uses "Health Information Custodian" and "agent" terminology; HIA uses "custodian" and "information manager." Zorva is Alberta-first; PHIPA templates are on request for Ontario pilots.

PIPEDA — Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act

Canada's federal private-sector privacy law. Zorva covers PIPEDA in addition to HIA / PHIPA / HIPAA depending on jurisdiction. The patient_hash salt design follows PIPEDA's "de-identify at the earliest practical point" requirement.

SOMB — Schedule of Medical Benefits

Alberta's billing code reference. The 18 AHCIP rules in v12 cite SOMB rule numbers and GR references. The SOMB is the source of truth for what AHCIP will and won't pay.

SFTP — SSH File Transfer Protocol

The Zorva pilot accepts 837P / CSV / paste-form. For clinics that prefer file drop, SFTP upload is available. Zorva does not require SFTP for the pilot.

837P — Professional 837P claim format

The standard electronic claim format for professional services. 837P is the most common Zorva input. The Zorva auditor reads the encounter header (NM1*85, NM1*87, HI, DTP*472) and the service lines (LX, SV1).

ROI calculator

For clinic decision-makers: how much revenue will Zorva save? Numbers are conservative. The default catch rate (0.77) is the v12 AHCIP val-set recall — the fraction of would-be billing errors the auditor catches. We use recall, not F1, because the cost of a missed error (revenue lost) is much higher than the cost of a false positive (5 seconds to dismiss). See README.md for the full recall/precision/F1 breakdown.

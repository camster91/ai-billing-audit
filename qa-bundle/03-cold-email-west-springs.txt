Three patterns I see on real Alberta family-medicine claims that probably show up in a 6-10 physician SW Calgary practice with a walk-in component:

- **Modifier-25 missing on E/M + procedure same day.** Cryo / biopsy / IUD insertion / joint injection billed the same day as an office visit without a -25 on the 03.04A line. AHCIP bundles the visit into the procedure. About $50 per miss, easily several per week in a busy walk-in component. Zorva catches it before submission.
- **EM undercode on comprehensive visits.** A complex annual physical with multiple chronic conditions, medication adjustment, and a referral — billed as 03.01A (brief) because the day-sheet template defaulted to it. The work was comprehensive; the code should match. About $30 per visit, again several per week. Zorva flags the mismatch.
- **CMGP modifier missed on chronic-disease visits.** T2DM / HTN / dyslipidemia / CKD / COPD / CHF patients on a 03.04A without the chronic disease management general premium modifier. About $25 per visit. Zorva fires only when the note documents a qualifying chronic condition.

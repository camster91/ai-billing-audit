Three patterns I see on real Alberta claims that almost certainly show up across the Red Deer PCN corridor — based on SOMB rule patterns the v12 Zorva auditor catches:

- **90-day post-op global window.** An 8-day post-cholecystectomy follow-up billed as 03.04A. AHCIP bundles that into the surgical fee per SOMB GR 3.2.1. The clinic gets paid once, then the post-payment audit recoups. About $50 per occurrence.
- **Annual physical billed as 03.04A.** Annual health maintenance visits for adults are largely non-insured under AHCIP. Billing them as insured and getting denied is the worst of both worlds. Catching it lets the biller route to Blue Cross or private billing and recover the full visit fee.
- **Same-day 03.04A + 03.05A conflict.** A comprehensive and a minor on the same date is a same-day conflict per SOMB — the 03.05A line is auto-denied. Catching it also surfaces the missed CMGP (chronic disease management) modifier on the chronic-disease patient (~$25 per visit).

Three patterns the v12 Zorva auditor catches on real Alberta claims that almost certainly show up across the Strathcona / Sherwood Park PCN network:

- **Modifier-25 missing on E/M + procedure same day.** A 03.04A billed the same day as a cryo or joint injection without a -25 on the E/M line. AHCIP bundles the visit into the procedure and pays the lower of the two. About $50 per miss, easily several per week in a 60-to-80-physician network.
- **CMGP modifier missed on chronic-disease visits.** Established T2DM, HTN, or dyslipidemia patients on a 03.04A without the chronic disease management general premium modifier. About $25 per visit left on the table. The rule fires only when the note documents a qualifying chronic condition (so it's not over-called) — this is the part v11 got wrong and v12 fixed.
- **Annual physical billed as insured.** Annual health maintenance visits for adults are largely non-insured under AHCIP. Catching it lets the biller route to Blue Cross or private billing and recover the full visit fee instead of getting denied.

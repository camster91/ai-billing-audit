Three patterns I see on Alberta claims all the time. They probably show up across the Red Deer PCN corridor too — I'm happy to confirm against your real claims:

1. Post-op follow-ups inside the 90-day global window. A 8-day post-cholecystectomy visit billed as 03.04A. AHCIP bundles that into the surgical fee per SOMB GR 3.2.1. The clinic gets paid once, then the post-payment audit recoups the second billing. About $50 lost each time it happens.

2. Annual physicals billed as insured 03.04A. Annual health maintenance visits for adults are largely not insured under AHCIP. When the clinic bills them as insured, two things go wrong: AHCIP denies the line, and the clinic loses the chance to bill Blue Cross or the patient directly for the full visit fee.

3. Same-day 03.04A + 03.05A. A comprehensive and a minor on the same date is a same-day conflict per SOMB. The 03.05A line is auto-denied. Catching it also surfaces a missed CMGP modifier on the chronic-disease patient — about $25 per visit.

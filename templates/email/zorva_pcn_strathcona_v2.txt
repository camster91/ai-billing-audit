Three patterns I see on Alberta claims often enough that I'd bet money at least one is costing Strathcona PCN money every month. Happy to confirm against your real claims:

1. Post-op follow-ups billed inside the 90-day global window. The 03.04A is auto-denied because AHCIP bundles it into the surgical fee. About $50 per occurrence.

2. Annual physicals billed as insured visits. Adult health-maintenance visits are largely not insured under AHCIP. The clinic loses the line to denial AND loses the chance to bill Blue Cross or the patient for the full fee.

3. Same-day 03.04A + 03.05A. The 03.05A is denied because the comprehensive visit already covers the work. Catching the conflict also surfaces a missed CMGP modifier on chronic-disease patients — about $25 per visit.
